Our Story

Built on craftsmanship.

Nusrat O Rehmat Traders was founded by Zahid Salman in 2022 with a simple goal: to craft sportswear that teams and athletes could truly rely on.

Based in Chak Qazi, Sialkot — a city with a long legacy of sports manufacturing — we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques.

Whether you need one custom jersey or a hundred team kits, every order gets the same attention to detail.
